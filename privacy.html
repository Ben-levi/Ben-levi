Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy applies to “Customer Manager,” a development and testing project operated by Ben Levi, a freelance developer based in Israel (“I,” “me”).

1. What this project does

The project is a messaging assistant that helps small businesses handle appointment bookings through WhatsApp. It is currently in a development and testing stage and is not available to the general public.

2. Information I may process

During testing, the project may process:

  • A phone number and WhatsApp display name of a test participant
  • The content of messages sent to the test number
  • Appointment details entered during testing (such as dates, times, and service type)

Only a small number of invited testers take part. I do not collect information from the general public.

3. How the information is used

Information is used only to operate and test the booking assistant, such as replying to messages and recording test appointments. I do not use it for advertising or marketing, and I do not send unsolicited messages.

4. Sharing and service providers

I do not sell or rent personal information. Information is processed only through the services needed to run the project: WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta), a cloud hosting service, and a database service. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Test data is kept only as long as needed for development. You can ask me to delete any information related to you at any time by emailing philepin@gmail.com, and I will do so within a reasonable time.

6. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect information, including restricted access to the systems that hold it. No system is completely secure.

7. Your rights

You may ask what information I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it.

8. Changes

I may update this policy and will change the date above when I do.
